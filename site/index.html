Why a supervisor is required: a background heartbeat cannot stop an unrelated process. The installed reference supervisor gates worker start, validates authority age, contains its cooperative process groups, and only then publishes terminal release, loss, uncertainty, or error.

What happens when an agent dies?

Silence has an expiry.

Heartbeats stop. The short lease expires. A waiting process can receive a higher term and decide whether to continue.

The term is the stale-writer defense: pass it downstream where the destination can reject an older generation.

Keep the boundary small

OctoStore coordinates authority.

  • One current coordinator
  • One temporary task owner
  • Short leases and renewal guidance
  • Monotonic terms and observable loss

Your agent system does the work.

  • Task discovery and delegation
  • Prompts, tools, branches, and queues
  • Human approval and policy
  • Idempotency and downstream fencing
